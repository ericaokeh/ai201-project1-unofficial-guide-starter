Italian Greyhound
Italian Greyhound
Description
Description
The Italian Greyhound, a miniature companion to the
Greyhound
, is a small, slender, breed with a knack for darting after small prey. The breed can be traced back to the Roman Empire, however, the breed rose to popularity in Renaissance Italy when owning a miniature version of a well-known breed was a demarcation of wealth and nobility. Many famous leaders have had their laps graced by an Italian Greyhound. For what the Italian Greyhound may lack in size, it does not lack the speed that full-size Greyhounds are known for. While elegant and graceful, the Italian Greyhound still makes for a playful pet; just make sure to have him don a sweater in the winter months to stay warm.
Dimensions & Sizes
Dimensions & Sizes
Italian Greyhounds have a withers height between 13”-15” (33-38 cm) and a typical weight in the range of 7-14 lb (3-6 kg). The Italian Greyhound has an overall body length of roughly 16”-19” (41-48 cm), standing height between 17”-20” (43-51 cm), and a typical lifespan of 12-15 years.
